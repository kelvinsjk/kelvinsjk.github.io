Hermes Calendar

A private assistant that reads and updates its owner's Google Calendar. It is for personal use and isn't offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Hermes Calendar accesses Google Calendar data only to answer the owner's requests and prepare their daily briefs. Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Calendar details are sent to the AI model provider that generates the assistant's replies, and are not sold or shared with anyone else. To remove access, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
